Kinabihagan Beach Resort

An unhurried stretch of coast,
where the day moves slower.

Tucked along the shoreline of Barangay Sua in Barobo, Kinabihagan is an affordable beachfront destination for couples, families, barkada, and larger groups. Cottages sit close to the water, coconut palms lean over the sand, and the sea stays visible from almost everywhere you settle.

Beachfront setting Coconut grove For groups of every size
01

Days here begin with the sound of the sea and end with the sky turning gold over the Pacific.

The Beach

Salt air, open water,
and time that stretches.

There is space here to swim, to rest, to sit under the palms, and to simply be with the people you came with. No crowds, no rush — just the coast as it has always been.
